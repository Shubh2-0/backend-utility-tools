# Buffer Setup — LinkedIn + Twitter Cross-Posting

**Why Buffer:** LinkedIn API is closed to individuals. Direct automation = account ban risk. Buffer is the "officially approved" middleware that LinkedIn allows.

**Cost:** Free tier (3 channels, 10 queued posts each). Sufficient for daily posting.

---

## Step 1 — Sign up (2 min)

1. Go to https://buffer.com
2. Click "Get Started" → sign up with Google (fastest)
3. Choose "Free" plan
4. Verify email if asked

---

## Step 2 — Connect LinkedIn (2 min)

1. On Buffer dashboard, click "Connect channel"
2. Select **LinkedIn**
3. Choose "Personal profile" (NOT Company Page)
4. LinkedIn OAuth popup appears → click "Allow"
5. Buffer now shows your LinkedIn as a channel ✅

---

## Step 3 — Connect Twitter/X (2 min)

Same flow:
1. "Connect channel" → **Twitter / X**
2. OAuth popup → "Authorize app"
3. Twitter is now connected ✅

---

## Step 4 — (Optional) Connect Instagram

Buffer requires:
- Instagram **Business** or **Creator** account (free to switch)
- Instagram linked to a **Facebook Page**

Steps if you want this:
1. Instagram app → Settings → Switch to Professional account
2. Connect to a Facebook page
3. In Buffer → "Connect channel" → Instagram → OAuth

(Skip if Instagram is personal-only.)

---

## Step 5 — Set posting schedule (3 min)

1. Click any connected channel → "Schedule" tab
2. Set times:
   - **LinkedIn:** 9 AM, 1 PM (Mon/Wed/Fri only)
   - **Twitter:** 9 AM, 1 PM, 8 PM (every day)
3. Save

**Why these times for India audience:**
- 9 AM = commute scroll
- 1 PM = lunch break
- 8 PM = evening scroll

Buffer will auto-post your queued items at these slots.

---

## Step 6 — Queue 10 posts (5 min)

1. Click "Queue" tab
2. Click "Create Post"
3. Open `linkedin-posts.md` → copy Post #1
4. Paste into Buffer composer
5. Select channel = LinkedIn
6. Click "Add to Queue"
7. **Repeat for Posts #2-10**

You've now queued ~3 weeks of LinkedIn content. Same flow for Twitter:
1. Open `twitter-posts.md`
2. Queue 10 tweets for Twitter channel

---

## Step 7 — Verify queue

Click "Queue" tab to see all scheduled posts with dates.

Buffer auto-posts at your scheduled times. If you want to:
- **Edit a post:** Click it → edit → save
- **Reschedule:** Drag to new slot
- **Delete:** Three-dot menu → delete

---

## Step 8 — Daily 5-min loop

Every Monday morning (5 min):
1. Open Buffer
2. Check queue is full for the week
3. If empty, queue 5-10 new posts from your markdown files

That's it. Buffer handles the actual posting.

---

## When NOT to use Buffer

- **Reactive posts** (responding to news, trending topics) — manual
- **Comments + replies** — ALWAYS manual (algorithm rewards this)
- **Live engagement** — manual
- **DMs** — manual (auto-DMing = ban)

Buffer is for SCHEDULED content. Live engagement stays human.

---

## Alternatives if Buffer doesn't work for you

| Tool | Free tier | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hootsuite** | 30-day trial then paid | More features but expensive |
| **Later** | Free for 30 posts/month | Strong on Instagram |
| **Publer** | Free for 3 channels | Decent alternative |
| **SocialPilot** | 14-day trial | Cheaper than Hootsuite |

Buffer is recommended for the simplicity + reliable free tier.

---

## Final tip: post quality > tool

Buffer ensures consistency. Your content quality drives engagement.

- ✅ Post Personal stories with specific numbers
- ✅ Ask questions that drive comments
- ❌ "5 tips for success" generic listicles
- ❌ AI-obvious posts

The 30 posts in `linkedin-posts.md` are written specifically to feel human. Stick with them or write your own — but don't paste GPT-default text.
